Method and sources
  • SPX forward is the median of K + exp(rT) × (call price − put price) for up to 11 near-forward strikes with positive call and put prices. Prices come from the Parquet's call_price and put_price fields. Their bid/ask and exchange quote times are unavailable.
  • SPX European IV uses the computed forward, the OTM quote at each strike and the Black–76 formula. ES IV is solved from its bid/ask midpoint. The dashboard also solves ES bid and ask IV with the same model.
  • Match exact snapshot instants and map by log(K/F). Interpolate linearly in SPX variance between bracketing strikes. The selected SPX IV is carried to ES maturity and repriced with ES time and the chosen futures price. For American exercise, add the early-exercise premium from a 400-step CRR futures tree to the Black–76 price.
  • Use year fractions of actual elapsed seconds divided by 365 days. Calendar DTE in the selectors is measured at the first ES snapshot for the selected contract, using exchange-local dates. Exact DTE appears in snapshot diagnostics. Raw SPX end-of-day markers in this feed are interpreted as the calendar day before settlement for recognized vendor series. Override the date and clock if your feed differs.
  • Positive ΔIV and mid − model mean ES is above the SPX-implied level. In spread means the model price falls within the ES bid and ask. Statistics use all matched strike-hours with equal weight per quote. The quoted spread and one session alone provide limited evidence about longer-run differences.
